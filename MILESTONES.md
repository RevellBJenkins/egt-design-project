# Project Milestones

## Phase 1 – Research and Problem Definition
- Identified user problem and project goals
- Conducted initial market and concept research
- Defined engineering requirements and constraints

---

## Phase 2 – Concept Development
- Generated multiple mechanical concepts
- Compared concepts using decision matrices
- Evaluated portability, usability, and manufacturability

---

## Phase 3 – System Design
- Selected electrical components and power architecture
- Analyzed battery and motor requirements
- Defined subsystem interfaces and overall system layout

---

## Phase 4 – Prototype Development
- Constructed prototype components
- Integrated electrical and mechanical subsystems
- Conducted preliminary functional testing

---

## Phase 5 – Evaluation and Documentation
- Evaluated overall system performance
- Documented design tradeoffs and engineering decisions
- Prepared presentation materials and final reports
- Organized repository and technical documentation
